# Credit Memo — Example

## Borrower Overview
The borrower is a mid-size industrial firm. This section states the borrower name and the industry. It also states the requested loan amount.

## Financial Analysis
This section reports the annual revenue and the net operating income. It reports the annual debt service. It states the DSCR and compares it to policy.

## Collateral
This section reports the appraised value of the collateral. It states the loan-to-value ratio.

## Risk Rating
This section reviews the guarantor and the guarantor net worth. It notes the environmental review status of the site.
